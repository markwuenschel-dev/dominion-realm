# Evolve and systematize the visual identity (don't reinvent)

The existing visual identity — deep blue-black palette, bone text, gold accent, spectral gradient, film grain, ambient glow, scroll reveals — is kept and "leveled up" (richer typography, more sophisticated layout, more art direction, more deliberate motion), not reinvented. As part of the overhaul it is formalized into reusable design tokens and components so the new page types inherit it consistently rather than re-implementing styles ad hoc.

The identity is distinctive and working; reinventing it would discard hard-won brand equity for risk. Recorded here so a future "let's redesign from scratch" impulse meets a deliberate decision rather than a vacuum.

Considered and rejected: keeping the identity untouched (under-delivers on "overhaul") and a from-scratch reinvention (highest effort and risk).
