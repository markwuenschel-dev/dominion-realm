On Earth, Mathias kept networks and distributed systems alive — the person who
understands that the failure is never the one part that broke, but the way the
parts depend on each other. In the Realm that instinct makes him a scout and a
confidant: the one who maps how things connect and where a failure will spread.

He is the warm, curious center of gravity for a group of difficult people. With
Marcus especially he is invaluable — his open, unhurried curiosity lets Marcus
talk his way to a conclusion without ever feeling managed into it.

His real gift surfaces when the threats arrive. Where others see scattered
attacks, Mathias sees one distributed thing wearing many bodies — and reads, in
the way a place behaves, the seam that everyone else has walked past.

## Wonder first

Where the others are driven by a wound, Mathias is driven by wonder — curiosity
that arrives before caution and mostly gets away with it. His grown-up problem
isn't fear, it's *continuance*: when he moves on, does the bond stay open, does the
road stay walkable behind him? He is the group's natural referee, and the only
thing that breaks his calm is watching trust get deliberately weaponized.

In the Realm his interface is the **Enteric Lattice** — a gut-deep sense for the
connection between people, places, and presences, and a way of reaching things
that stay closed to everyone else. If Marcus's attention feels intense, Mathias's
feels safe; people tell him things without deciding to.

</RevealGate>

<RevealGate tier="deep">

## The door that opens because he knocks politely

The dark edge of Mathias's gift is that things open to him *because he approaches
them correctly* — a place, a presence, a ruin, a portal — and staying in contact
risks changing him into something that fits through the opening. At the finale he
is **injured, not killed**, in the act of detecting and proving the Xyloryn
ruins-portal threat before anyone else will believe it. In the long run he stays
behind as Eriadne's route-keeper and emissary — the one who holds the roads open —
while Marcus and Serra climb toward the far end of the story. He survives Book One.

</RevealGate>
