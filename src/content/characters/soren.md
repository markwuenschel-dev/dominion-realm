Earth's most gifted gamers were recruited for an experiment they barely
understood — a neuroquantum lattice threaded directly into the optic nerve. When
Soren woke, he was no longer on Earth. He was in the Realm, and his implant
rendered it as RPG logic: health bars, skill trees, loot, levels. It was
legible. It was survivable.

But the power humming beneath the interface is not code, and it was not built for
him. It is ancient, and it is real — and the deeper he descends, the more the
abstraction frays.
