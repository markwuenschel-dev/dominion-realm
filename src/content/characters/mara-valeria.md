Mara watches. It is the thing she is best at and the thing she hides behind — a
creative professional on Earth, somewhere in the blend of visual art, design, and
writing, with the habit of noticing everything while drawing no notice to
herself.

In the field she is the setup to Serra's strike. Serra reads the weak point by
feel and goes; Mara has already arranged the moment so the opening exists. The
two have been tandem partners long enough that the shorthand between them borders
on telepathy: Mara watches, Serra moves, and the gap between those two things is
where the kill happens.

Her interface is authorship of *salience* — what a mind notices and what it slides
past. She can make the overlooked impossible to ignore, or make herself the thing
no one quite registers. Where Marcus treats people as systems to be solved, Mara
is the standing reminder that a person is not a variable, a puzzle, or a resource.
