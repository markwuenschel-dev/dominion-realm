Field observation, descent day forty-one.

The satchel beside the body glows **common** — that soft, dismissible green the
lattice paints over things not worth my time. Three copper, a whetstone, a heel
of bread gone to stone. The interface has already appraised it, sorted it,
filed it under *take or leave*.

What the glow does not say: that the satchel was packed by someone who expected
to come home. The bread was wrapped twice against the damp. The whetstone is
worn on one side only, the way a right-handed person wears it. There is a
child's drawing folded into the seam, and the interface has no color for that
at all, so it shows me nothing — a blank where a whole life should be.

Green for common. The Realm keeps no such category. Down here everything is
rare, because down here everything was *someone's*. I take the copper. I leave
the drawing where it was folded. The glow does not change. It never does.
