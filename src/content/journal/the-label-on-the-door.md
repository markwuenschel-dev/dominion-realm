The door at the end of the cistern reads **Locked (Lv. 12)** in the soft amber
the interface reserves for things it would rather I not touch yet. A number. A
threshold. A polite suggestion that I come back stronger.

But I put my hand to the wood and it is warm, and the grain runs in spirals no
carpenter cut. The lattice does not have a word for *warm wood that remembers*.
So it gives me a level instead — twelve — and lets me believe the obstacle is
arithmetic.

I am beginning to suspect the numbers are not measurements. They are
translations. The Realm says something true and ancient, and the implant, doing
its best, hands me back the nearest lie I can act on. *Locked* is not a state of
the door. It is a state of me.

I will come back at thirteen. I do not think the door will care.
