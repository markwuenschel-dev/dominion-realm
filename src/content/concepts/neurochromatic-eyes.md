The Neurochromatic Eyes are an *interface* — emergent, biological, and, above all,
**accurate**. They do not create power from nothing; they redistribute the body's
bandwidth, and emotional state decides what the eye prioritizes. The pupil is the
aperture, the iris the filter, the limbal ring the warning light. What the eye
shows a walker is true. The danger was never in the seeing.

There are six stages, each costing more than the last: **Limbal Shift**, **Iris
Refraction**, **Neuro-Optical Overdrive**, **Spectral Partition**, **Gaze
Interference**, and **Prism Coherence**. The earliest only reveal that the Realm
is layered. The last dissolve the interface entirely — perception of the substrate
whole and unmediated, which few minds survive intact. The cost is paid in the eye
itself: migraines, broken vessels, blood tears if it is pushed too far.

Do not confuse them with the **Eyes of Meszkhal**. The interface *perceives*; the
item *interprets*. One sees truly. The other only sounds certain.
