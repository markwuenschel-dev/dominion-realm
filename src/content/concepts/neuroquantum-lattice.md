Astria built the lattice to win a game. Threaded into the optic nerve, it
translates the Realm into the only grammar its recruits trusted: health bars,
skill trees, loot, levels. For most walkers it is a tool. For Marcus it is
something closer to a second self — the membrane through which he survives contact
with a world that was never designed to be understood.

It is a crucial point of canon that the lattice is **not** a power source. It
creates nothing. It is a *receiver and translator* — the Realm has real
metaphysical laws, and the implant reads them as signal and renders them as UI,
because the wearer's mind is a gamer's mind and reaches for that frame. Strip the
interface away and the power is still there. It was always there.

The deeper truth the lattice hides is the one the whole story turns on: the
interface is not the world. It is a translation, and every translation leaves
something out. What it leaves out does not stop being real for having gone unread
— and a mind that mistakes the readout for the territory is a mind the Realm can
reach.
