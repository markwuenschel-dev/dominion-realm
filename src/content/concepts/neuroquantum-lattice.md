Earth built the lattice to win a game. Threaded into the optic nerve, it
translates the Realm into the only grammar its recruits trusted: health bars,
skill trees, loot, levels. For most walkers it is a tool. For Soren it is
something closer to a second self — the membrane through which he survives
contact with a world that was never designed to be understood.

The deeper truth the lattice hides is the one the whole story turns on: the
interface is not the world. It is a translation, and every translation leaves
something out. What it leaves out does not stop being real for having gone
unread — and a mind that mistakes the readout for the territory is a mind the
Realm can reach.
