Astria is the institution on Earth that built the neuroquantum lattice and ran
the experiment that put six gifted gamers — Marcus among its own engineers —
into the Realm. What Astria wanted, what it understood about where it was sending
them, and how much of the crossing was accident versus design is the thread this
entry will follow.

Marcus worked for them before he wore their hardware. That detail is the hook:
the protagonist helped build the thing that translated a real metaphysical world
into the interface he now mistakes for the truth.
