The Nightbound are seven extraordinarily capable individuals — exiles, scholars,
survivors, apostates — gathered beneath one founder's conclusion: that
civilizations far greater than today's cosmic powers once faced the N'hal and died,
because they prepared for the wrong war, leaning on their most refined systems —
exactly the structures the N'hal unmake. The current powers are repeating the
mistake. Someone, the founder holds, must prepare differently, before consensus is
even possible.

Their conviction is not *that they deserve to decide the future.* It is that **no
one else will in time.** They are not a cosmic empire and not would-be rulers — a
small, lawful, hierarchical order with internal discipline and a coherent plan,
willing to violate consent, law, and sovereignty in service of it.

They are the hidden hand behind the crossing. The Nightbound conceived and funded
the operation that pulled six elite Earth gamers into the Realm — selected not as
foretold heroes but as **keys**, each compatible with one of six interfaces the
work will need. They are anti-villains whose most dangerous trait is that their
reasoning is coherent, and that they can point to the dead.
