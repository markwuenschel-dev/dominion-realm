The Realm Walkers are not merely travelers. They move through and between
interface realities without being fully claimed by any single one, and they appear
to oppose any one cosmic philosophy becoming total — balance-keepers in a cosmos
of absolutists. They discover, warn, witness, and intervene, but they do so as
individuals rather than as an empire.

That is the heart of their quarrel with the Nightbound. To the Nightbound, the
Walkers are brave and useless: *witnesses are not enough.* To the Walkers, the
Nightbound turn people into tools and call it preparation. Both critiques are true,
which is what keeps the conflict honest.

Marcus may belong among them without knowing it — because his implant-and-interface
combination lets him read the translation layers others mistake for reality, the
exact thing a Walker does. Late in Book One, drawn by a breach in the world, one of
them witnesses what he can do, and comes to find him.
