The Xyloryn is the Realm's answer to confidence. It is alive, and it is
patient, and it remembers. A tactic that works against it works exactly once;
by the second engagement the swarm has metabolized the lesson and grown a
counter. Walkers who survive it do so by never repeating themselves.

It is the kind of threat the interface is good at: countable, mappable, beatable
— if you stay inventive. That legibility is precisely why it is the lesser of
the Realm's two endings.
