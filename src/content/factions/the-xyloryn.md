The Xyloryn do not worship evolution. They worship **pressure** — *a species
reveals itself only when challenged.* Their worlds are not beautiful or efficient;
they are testing grounds. Their war loop is **Observe → Consume → Learn → Adapt →
Cultivate**: they treat a world as a living problem, consume it to understand it,
reshape it to force life into new adaptive states, then file the result and move
on. To the swarm, war is the universe asking life a question — *what survives when
survival is no longer comfortable?*

A tactic works against them exactly once. By the next engagement the swarm has
metabolized the lesson and grown a counter; walkers who survive it do so by never
repeating themselves. But the swarm imitates the *surface* of a thing — it grows
organs that mimic an interface, never the thing the interface truly is. It depends
on command-nodes, and its elite **Myrmidons** are grown only after it has gathered
battlefield data, each one a local answer to the defenders it has studied.

That legibility is the point: the Xyloryn are countable, mappable, beatable — if
you stay inventive. A single Myrmidon can nearly end a party of six. The swarm is
the apocalypse you can prepare for. It is the lesser of the Realm's two endings.
