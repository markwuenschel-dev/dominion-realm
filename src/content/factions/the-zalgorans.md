The Xyloryn can be fought. The Zalgorans cannot be read. They are not of the
Realm's substrate so much as a wound in it — and proximity to them does not lower
a walker's health so much as corrode the translation the lattice depends on.

Numbers stutter. Names slip. The interface that makes the Realm survivable is the
first thing to go, and a walker who has only ever trusted the readout finds, too
late, that there is nothing underneath it they know how to hold. They are the
ending the Eyes of Meszkhal were meant for — and the reason the Eyes cost so much.
